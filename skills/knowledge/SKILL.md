# Preserve verified knowledge

## When to use

Use when a lesson may need to outlive the current task. Follow AX-03, AX-05, AX-06, and AX-07.

## Procedure

1. Identify the responsibility and search the relevant documentation, skill, test, rule, or tool for its owner. Prefer a small correction at that source when it can preserve the verified behavior.
2. Keep temporary observations and unresolved hypotheses in task evidence. Confirm a durable procedure with reproducible evidence and state when it applies.
3. Choose the smallest appropriate scope. Record the owner, purpose, and maintenance lifecycle for a new artifact. Keep repeated guidance as references to its authoritative source.
4. Preserve the governing rules and required checks. Route proposed changes to shared fundamentals through their owners and independent review with an explicit version and scope.
5. Verify the revised procedure or check against the demonstrated problem and references used by affected contributors.

## Expected evidence

Identify the authoritative source inspected, underlying observation, confirming evidence, changed scope, and responsible owner. Record what remains temporary and what verified guidance was updated.
